# Off-site & Human Tasks (agents plan, humans execute)

## Must-do (highest ROI)
1. **Google Business Profile** for the Geneva shop: categories, hours, address, products, photos weekly, posts, Q&A. Owner: client.
2. **Reviews**: ask every customer (QR at the till, post-purchase email). Google first; add a review platform later. Never buy or fake reviews.
3. **Swiss directories**: local.ch, search.ch, relevant health/fitness directories; identical NAP.
4. **Partnerships**: gyms, sports clubs, coaches, physiotherapists, running/CrossFit groups → mentions and links, referral codes.
5. **Guest posts / digital PR**: Swiss fitness, health and lifestyle sites. 1–2 per week; angle: original data (price per serving comparisons, dosage guides), expert quotes.
6. **Brand/supplier pages**: ask each brand to list nutrifitness.ch as a Swiss retailer ("où acheter").
7. **Creators**: send samples to local fitness creators; reviews create links and AI citations.
8. **Forums / Reddit / Facebook groups / YouTube**: genuine, disclosed participation; answer questions with useful detail.

## Weekly cadence
| Day | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Approve weekly plan from the Orchestrator | You |
| Tue | Approve content batch | You + client |

## Targets (60 days)
15–30 quality referring domains · 30+ new Google reviews · GBP complete and active · 5+ partner/brand mentions · all products live in Merchant Center.

## Supply to agents
Label photos or data per product (`data/nutrifitness-product-data-collection.xlsx`), real customer questions, shop photos, staff bios/credentials, delivery and return policy text, opening hours.
