# Content & Page Templates

## Global rules
Swiss-French, natural tone. CHF and local shipping references. Answer-first intro (40–60 words). One H1. No H1 inside descriptions. No manufacturer copy-paste. No health or medical claims. Unknown fact = `[À VÉRIFIER]`. Every page links to relevant categories, products and guides. Add FAQs from real customer questions.

## Product name
`Product type + variant + size – Brand` (≤ 70 characters; no ALL CAPS, emojis or promo words; no abbreviations like CAPS/TABS).
Example: `Créatine monohydrate 250 g – Applied Nutrition`.

## Short description (40–60 words)
What it is → who it is for → key spec → one real differentiator.

## Long description (250–500 words, HTML without H1)
1. Summary (2–3 sentences)
2. Pour qui ?
3. Caractéristiques clés (bullets: dose per serving, servings, flavour, vegan/lactose-free, certifications)
4. Conseils d'utilisation (from the label)
5. Composition et valeurs nutritionnelles (table per serving; ingredients; allergens)
6. Conservation, origine, mentions obligatoires
7. Acheter chez Nutrifitness (Geneva shop, delivery in Switzerland)
8. Questions fréquentes (3–5 real questions, H3)
Also add: price per serving (calculated), comparison link to a guide, disclaimer line ("Les compléments alimentaires ne remplacent pas une alimentation variée et équilibrée.").

## Meta
Title ≤ 60 characters (`Product – Brand | Nutrifitness` when it fits). Description ≤ 155 characters with product, key spec, Geneva shop/Swiss delivery.

## Category page
H1; 150–300 words intro above the product grid (target keyword + intent); server-rendered product links; unique FAQ (5–8 Qs); buying-guide link; BreadcrumbList + ItemList + FAQPage schema.

## Blog / guide
Author byline with bio and credentials; "last updated"; answer-first intro; table of contents; comparison tables; original insight (dosage, price per serving, staff advice); sources; FAQ; internal links to products and categories. Target 1,200–2,000 words unless the intent needs less.

## Local page `/magasin-geneve/`
NAP identical to Google Business Profile, hours, map, transport/parking, photos, staff, reviews, services, LocalBusiness schema.

## Claim policy
Allowed: factual product description, composition, usage from the label, standard, already-authorised performance wording validated by the client. Not allowed: disease prevention/treatment, hormone or cholesterol effects, "détox", "fat burning" promises, numeric performance promises ("jusqu'à 10 %"), neurological benefits. The Compliance agent blocks and flags; a human decides.

## Catalogue clean-up (from the audit)
Merge duplicate category trees (PERTE DE POIDS vs COMPLÉMENTS > PERTE DE POIDS & MÉTABOLISME; PROTÉINES vs COMPLÉMENTS > PROTÉINES & GAINERS). One primary category per product. Keep "Best-sellers" for ~8–12 real top sellers. Fix wrong categories flagged in the workbook. Complete missing brands, GTINs, short/long descriptions. Remove Instagram links from body text and replace them with internal links.
