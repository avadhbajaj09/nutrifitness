|---|---|---|

## KPIs (weekly report)
- Indexed vs submitted pages · Merchant approved products · disapprovals
- GSC impressions, clicks, CTR, average position per keyword cluster
- Keywords in top 3 / top 10 / top 20
- Core Web Vitals pass rate
- Referring domains gained · GBP views, calls, directions · review count
- AI-citation rate across the tracked prompt set (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Mode)
- Organic sessions, orders, revenue
- Open issues by severity; agent cost per week

## Realistic expectations
Weeks 1–5 are mostly foundation. Meaningful ranking movement typically appears from week 6 onward on brand/product and low-difficulty long-tail queries. Competitive head terms (e.g. "whey protéine") usually take longer than 60 days.
